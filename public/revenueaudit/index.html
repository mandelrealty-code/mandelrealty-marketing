Superhost & Guest Favorite, by the numbers

Real Airbnb criteria, plus what each badge is actually worth.

Superhost

Source: Airbnb Help, Article 829
  • 10+ completed reservations, or 3 reservations totaling 100+ nights, in the trailing 12 months
  • 90%+ response rate — respond or accept/decline within 24 hours
  • Less than a 1% cancellation rate
  • 4.8+ overall rating
  • Account in good standing, reviewed quarterly (Jan/Apr/Jul/Oct 1st)

Guest Favorite

Source: Airbnb's own hosting resources
  • At least 5 reviews in the past 4 years, with 1+ in the past 2 years
  • High ratings across all six categories: check-in, cleanliness, accuracy, communication, location, value
  • Cancellations and quality-related service issues under 1% on average
  • A low review-removal rate
  • Recalculated daily, listing by listing

Free · common revenue leaks

What is quietly costing you bookings

The most common reasons a good property underperforms — almost none of them require a renovation to fix.

✕
Static pricing. A rate that never moves around weekends, local events, or seasonality leaves money on the table on the best nights and sits empty on the rest.
✕
Outdated photos or a flat listing title. Guests decide in seconds — a title and photo set that has not been touched in a year is losing clicks it does not know about.
✕
Slow responses or messy turnovers. Both show up in reviews, and reviews are the single biggest lever on Superhost and Guest Favorite status.
✕
Amenities you already have, sitting unused. A hot tub, sauna, gym, or private parking that is off-limits, hidden, or not mentioned in the listing is revenue nobody can pay for if they do not know it exists.
✕
One platform only. Airbnb-only listings leave real demand on VRBO, Booking.com, and direct bookings entirely unclaimed.
