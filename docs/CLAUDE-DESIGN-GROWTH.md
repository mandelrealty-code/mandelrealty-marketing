## Visual language for THIS plan (required)

Growth is the math / performance plan. The page must feel **cool and specific** through **editorial SaaS graphics**, not gimmicks.

**Do use (premium, flat, geometric):**
1. **Benchmark split diagram** — one horizontal or stacked bar: muted segment = “up to benchmark” (10% or 5%), green segment = “above benchmark” (35% or 45%). Labels, not icons with faces.
2. **Fee waterfall / calculation card** — clean typography layout for the illustrative $3,000 → $4,000 → $650 example (Aligned only). Hairline rules, monospace or IBM Plex Mono OK for numbers only.
3. **12-month benchmark ribbon** — subtle 12-tick seasonal strip (Jan–Dec) showing that benchmarks are monthly, not one flat yearly number. Abstract bars or dots in green/ink. No fake city charts claiming real data.
4. **Before / after fee share** — simple two-column compare: “Flat 20–25% on everything” vs “Low fee on your number + share only on growth.” Geometric only.
5. **Hero visual** — full-bleed STR interior with a **green-leaning colour grade** (plants, natural light, cool greens). Or a quiet abstract green gradient field behind a sharp product graphic. No cartoon illustrations.

**Do not use:**
- Clipart arrows, rocket ships, trophy badges, handshake cartoons, money bags
- 3D glossy icons, neon glow, confetti, isometric “startup” scenes
- Fake dashboards that look like Airbnb screenshots (proof numbers stay typographic / chart-ready for engineering)
- Purple, gold-as-plan-accent, or blue as the page accent (blue is Full Service)

Keep graphics **sparse**: 2–4 signature pieces total, each tied to a section. White space wins.

## Sections (one job each)

1. **Who it’s for**  
   Live hosts with booking history who want full ops without paying flat 20–25% on revenue they already earn. New empty listings → Full Service or Essentials. Optional small “qualifies / does not” two-column list.

2. **How the fee works** (signature graphic section)  
   Lead with the **benchmark split diagram**. Copy explains: up to Benchmark → lower fee; above → higher fee only on the upside. Gross = nightly + upsells; excludes cleaning / pass-throughs; HST on fees.

3. **Two tiers** — Aligned Growth **10%/35%** vs Confidence Partner **5%/45%**  
   Clean compare cards with green accent on emphasis. Short “who picks which.” Include the **fee waterfall card** for Aligned only: Benchmark $3,000 · actual $4,000 → ($3,000 × 10%) + ($1,000 × 35%) = $650 + HST. Label illustrative, not a guarantee.

4. **Benchmark Revenue**  
   Use the **12-month ribbon** graphic. Copy: seasonality built in; same calendar month averaged across history; thin history → fair comps; fixed for initial 12-month term; monthly statement within 7 days of month end.

5. **What’s included**  
   Same full-management scope (messaging, pricing, cleaning coordination, inventory, listing optimization, reviews, benchmark statement). Owner may keep their cleaner. Flat icon row OK only if icons are **simple line/geometry**, not cartoon.

6. **What this plan does not include**  
   Honest block: Furniture Investment is **not** available on Growth (links `/furniture` + `/full-service`). No invented furniture budgets.

7. **Proof**  
   `$26,995` all of 2025 → `$33,713` May–Aug 2026 · `+159%`. Toronto client, results vary. Chart-ready white card band (engineering will mount the interactive earnings chart in green). Leave mount-friendly empty structure or typographic stats.

8. **Fit checklist**  
   Live listing with history · authority to sign · building/city allows STR · open to a 15-minute call.

9. **FAQ**  
   Who qualifies · Aligned vs Confidence · how benchmark is set · HST · cleaning · furniture not available · early exit $999 / $499 · term 12 months then MTM · onboarding fee on call · areas / platforms.

10. **Final dark conversion band** `#222222`
