# MRG Growth Partnership Agreement — CRM talk track

Use this when a lead asks about the Growth Partnership, growth fee, lower base fee vs 20%, paying more only after we grow revenue, Aligned Growth, Confidence Partner, or benchmark pricing.

## What it is
Mandel Realty Group Growth Partnership is a performance-tied full management plan for hosts who already have a live short-term rental listing with booking history. MRG gets a smaller fee on revenue up to the host's own monthly benchmark, and a larger fee only on revenue above that benchmark.

Contact: info@mandelrealtygroup.com · (647) 381-7325 · Toronto.

## Who qualifies
- Property must have an existing live Airbnb (or other STR) listing with demonstrated booking history.
- MRG needs real past performance data to set Benchmark Revenue.
- Owner represents the listing has been actively booked for a period prior to start (filled in on the contract).
- Not for brand-new listings with no history — use Full Service / standard management or Managed Essentials instead.

## How fees work (two tiers — owner picks one)
Gross booking revenue = nightly rate + upsells. Excludes cleaning fees and other pass-through fees. HST applies on top of all fees.

### Aligned Growth
- Below / up to that month's Benchmark Revenue: 10% of monthly revenue
- Above Benchmark Revenue: 35% of monthly revenue

### Confidence Partner
- Below / up to that month's Benchmark Revenue: 5% of monthly revenue
- Above Benchmark Revenue: 45% of monthly revenue

Both tiers have a smaller floor than MRG's standard Full-Service ~20%–25% flat rate. MRG earns the larger share only on growth past the property's own established number.

### Example (Aligned Growth only — illustrative, not a guarantee)
Benchmark $3,000 · actual revenue $4,000 → fee = ($3,000 × 10%) + ($1,000 × 35%) = $300 + $350 = $650 + HST.

## Benchmark Revenue
- Set per calendar month (12 monthly figures), not one flat yearly number, so seasonality is built in.
- For each month: average gross booking revenue for that same calendar month across available years of history (e.g. January = average of all Januaries).
- If history is under 12 months or a month has no data, MRG sets a fair benchmark using comparable market analysis.
- Benchmarks are fixed for the initial 12-month term; can be re-set in writing on renewal.
- Fees calculated monthly; payout within 7 days of month end with an itemized benchmark statement.

## Services included
Same full scope as Full-Service management unless the owner keeps specific tasks:
- Guest communication & messaging
- Cleaning / turnover coordination
- Inventory management & restocking
- Dynamic pricing & listing optimization
- Review & reputation support
- Monthly benchmark performance statement

Owner can keep their own cleaner; MRG coordinates with them. Owner still pays their cleaner unless otherwise agreed. Insurance, taxes, maintenance/repairs stay Owner's responsibility (MRG may refer vendors).

## Term & exit
- Initial term: 12 months, then month-to-month.
- Non-renewal / cancel after initial term: 30 days written notice.
- Early termination during initial term: $999 if within first 6 months; $499 if after 6 months but still inside the initial 12 months.
- After termination, MRG remains entitled to the agreed fee on reservations booked during the term, and on reservations booked within 9 months after termination that relate to listings/systems MRG managed.

## Onboarding fee
Contract has an onboarding fee field; may be waived with MRG approval. Do not invent a dollar amount if not provided for that lead.

## Important exclusions / pairing rules
- Furniture Investment Program is NOT available with the Growth Partnership.
- Furniture Investment is only offered with Standard (20%) or Full Service (25%) management agreements.
- Do not promise furniture makeover budgets under Growth Partnership.

## How to talk about it to leads
- Lead with: lower fee on your current number; we only take a bigger cut on what we grow past your benchmark.
- Confirm they have a live listing with history before pitching Growth.
- Do not invent custom % outside Aligned Growth (10%/35%) or Confidence Partner (5%/45%).
- Do not guarantee revenue or specific dollar outcomes — examples are illustrative only.
- HST is extra.
- If they want fixed predictable cost with less full ops, point to Managed Essentials instead.
- If they want furniture investment, they need Standard 20% or Full Service 25%, not Growth.
