# MRG Managed Essentials Fixed-Rate Agreement — CRM talk track

Use this when a lead asks about Managed Essentials, a flat monthly management fee, Message & Book, Message & Optimize, $199, $349, fixed-rate help without full % of revenue, or keeping cleaning/maintenance themselves while MRG handles messaging/pricing.

## What it is
Managed Essentials is Mandel Realty Group's fixed monthly fee plan with a limited, defined scope. Unlike Full-Service / Growth Partnership (percentage of revenue), this is a predictable flat cost for owners who want to stay more hands-on and only offload specific tasks.

Contact: info@mandelrealtygroup.com · (647) 381-7325 · Toronto.

## Two plans (owner picks one)
Fees below exclude HST. HST applies on top.

### Message & Book — $199 / month
Includes:
- AI + virtual-assistant guest messaging
- Booking confirmations
- Guest ID verification & screening
- Review requests
- Monthly performance snapshot

### Message & Optimize — $349 / month
Everything in Message & Book, plus:
- Dynamic pricing management
- Scheduled listing & photo optimization
- Monthly report with recommended next steps

## What is NOT included (both plans)
Unless agreed in writing, Owner remains responsible for:
- Cleaning coordination and turnovers
- Maintenance arrangements and related costs
- Inventory management and restocking
- All property expenses including insurance and taxes

MRG may refer trusted vendors on request but does not arrange or pay for maintenance/repairs under this plan.

## Payment options
### Monthly, cancel anytime
- Billed at the standard monthly fee ($199 or $349)
- No minimum commitment
- Cancel with 30 days written notice

### 12-Month Commitment (financed)
- 12-month term financed through Klarna (or comparable provider designated by MRG)
- 5% discount off the standard monthly fee
- No refunds on this annual path — tradeoff for the discount
- Early termination follows the financing provider's terms; financed fees are not refundable
- Fees billed monthly in advance unless the financing arrangement says otherwise

## Plan changes
- Owner may upgrade Message & Book → Message & Optimize, or move to Full-Service / Growth Partnership, anytime by written request; new fees start next billing cycle.
- Downgrades / changes during a 12-Month Commitment stay subject to that financing commitment.

## Onboarding fee
Contract has an onboarding fee field; may be waived with MRG approval. Do not invent a dollar amount if not set for that lead.

## Access
Owner must give MRG the platform access needed (messaging; and pricing/listing access for Message & Optimize). Removing access can block delivery of the plan.

## How this compares for SMS replies
- Want predictable flat cost + keep cleaning/ops: Managed Essentials.
- Want full ops with % of revenue and growth upside: Growth Partnership (live listing + history) or Full Service (~20%–25%).
- Furniture Investment Program pairs with Standard 20% / Full Service 25%, not with Managed Essentials unless separately agreed — do not invent furniture terms here.

## How to talk about it to leads
- Lead with: fixed monthly fee, clear scope, you keep day-to-day control of cleaning/maintenance.
- Quote only $199 (Message & Book) or $349 (Message & Optimize) + HST.
- Mention optional Klarna 12-month path with ~5% off if they ask about annual / discount — no refunds on that path.
- Do not say Managed Essentials includes full turnover cleaning or repairs.
- Do not invent % management fees for this plan — it is fixed-rate, not revenue share.
