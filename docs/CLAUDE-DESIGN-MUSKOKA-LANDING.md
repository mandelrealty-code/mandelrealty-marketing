# Claude Design brief — `/muskoka` landing page (MRG)

**Product:** Mandel Realty Group — dedicated **Muskoka cottage / STR management** landing page  
**URL:** `mandelrealtygroup.com/muskoka`  
**Audience:** Muskoka cottage owners (Bracebridge, Gravenhurst, Huntsville, Lake of Bays, Port Carling, and surrounding lakes) who own or are buying a short-term rental and want hands-off hosting with stronger seasonal revenue  
**Your job:** Design a **complete, conversion-ready single-page landing** (desktop ~1280 + mobile ~390). Full creative freedom on visual style. Fill every section with real MRG offers and Muskoka-specific copy — not a generic property-management template.

---

## Creative freedom (important)

**Do NOT use MRG’s existing site palette** (no forced dark `#0a0a0a`, no gold `#c4a35a` requirement, no “match the homepage” rule).

You have **full creative freedom** to invent a distinctive visual system for this page only:

- **Direction:** Modern **glass morphism** as a core language — frosted panels, soft blur, translucent surfaces over rich Muskoka photography. Pair with expressive typography and atmospheric depth.
- Invent your own accent + background system. Lake light, granite, pine canopy, mist, dusk water reflections — let the *place* drive the look.
- **Brand first:** “Mandel Realty Group” (or a strong MRG wordmark treatment) must be a **hero-level** signal in the first viewport — not a tiny nav logo. If you strip the nav, the first screen should still clearly belong to MRG Muskoka.
- Prefer expressive display + clean body fonts. Avoid Inter / Roboto / Arial / system defaults.
- Background: never flat single-color. Use photography, gradient washes, glass layers, subtle grain/mist — atmosphere.
- **Full-bleed hero only.** Dominant edge-to-edge Muskoka property image or cinematic lake plane. No inset hero cards, side-panel heroes, rounded media tiles, collages, or floating image blocks in the first viewport.
- **Hero budget:** brand + one headline + one short supporting sentence + one CTA group + one dominant image. No stats, schedules, address blocks, or secondary promos in the first viewport.
- **No hero overlays:** no floating badges, promo stickers, chips, or callout boxes on top of hero media.
- **Cards:** default no cards. Cards only when they contain a real interaction (form, pricing picker, FAQ expand). Glass panels that are structural layout are fine; card soup is not.
- One job per section. Ship **2–3 intentional motions** (reveal, glass parallax, soft lake-light drift) — presence, not noise.
- Avoid AI-default looks: purple-on-white SaaS, cream+terracotta+serif broadsheet, neon glow piles, emoji, rounded-full pill clusters, multi-layer shadow stacks.

**Tone:** Premium cottage-country STR partner. Calm confidence. Local Muskoka fluency. Boutique (not franchise). Never “we guarantee X% more revenue.”

### Positioning for Muskoka
- Muskoka cottages are high-ADR, highly seasonal (summer peak, shoulder weekends, winter ice/ski demand). Owners burn out on turnovers, lake-road logistics, and “set and forget” managers who don’t reprice for long weekends / events.
- MRG sells **systems + guest experience → reviews → search visibility → more bookings → more money** — same chain as the main site, localized to cottage country.
- Boutique capacity: **we only partner with ~20 listings at a time** — scarcity is real; use it once, tastefully, not as spam.

### Offers to present (fill the “what we offer” / pricing area)

**1) Full Service / Standard management (~20%–25% of revenue)**  
Hands-off: guest messaging, cleaning/turnover coordination, inventory, dynamic pricing, listing optimization, review protection. Furniture Investment Program may pair with Standard (~20%) or Full Service (~25%) only.

**2) Growth Partnership** (existing live listing + booking history required)  
Performance-tied: lower fee up to the owner’s monthly Benchmark Revenue; larger fee only on revenue above that benchmark.  
- **Aligned Growth:** 10% up to benchmark / 35% above  
- **Confidence Partner:** 5% up to benchmark / 45% above  
Gross booking revenue = nightly + upsells; excludes cleaning/pass-throughs. HST on fees. Furniture Investment is **NOT** available with Growth.

**3) Managed Essentials** (fixed monthly — owner keeps cleaning/ops)  
- **Message & Book — $199/mo:** AI + VA messaging, booking confirmations, guest ID/screening, review requests, monthly snapshot  
- **Message & Optimize — $349/mo:** above + dynamic pricing + scheduled listing/photo optimization + monthly next-steps report  
HST extra. Optional Klarna 12-month path ~5% off (no refunds on that path).

**4) Furniture Investment / Airbnb makeover**  
Pitch as available with Standard/Full Service — refresh outdated cottage interiors so the listing can command Muskoka summer rates. Do **not** invent a dollar budget if not provided; stay high-level (“we invest in the makeover so the listing can earn”).
