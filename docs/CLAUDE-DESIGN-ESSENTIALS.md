## Visual language for THIS plan (required)

Essentials is the **clear-scope / fixed-price** plan. Feel cool and specific through **editorial SaaS graphics**, not gimmicks.

**Do use (premium, flat, geometric) — pick 2–4:**
1. **Scope split diagram** — two columns or a divided board: “MRG handles” (messaging, screening, confirmations; + pricing/optimization on $349) vs “You keep” (cleaning, maintenance, inventory, expenses). Orange accent on MRG side only.
2. **Price ladder** — typographic cards for **$199** and **$349** with hairline rules, IBM Plex Mono OK for the dollar amounts. Show what’s added at $349 as a short delta list, not a wall of text.
3. **Inbox timeline graphic** — quiet horizontal sequence: Enquiry → Screen → Confirm → Review request. Geometric nodes/lines in orange/ink. No chat-bubble cartoons.
4. **Flat vs % compare** — simple two-panel: “Fixed $199 / $349” vs “% of revenue (Full Service / Growth)”. Helps the owner choose without math theatre.
5. **Hero visual** — full-bleed STR interior with a **warm orange / amber colour grade** (sunset light, terracotta accents, warm wood). Different from blue-hour Full Service and green forest Growth. No text stickers on the photo.

**Do not use:** cartoon phones, robot mascots, sparkle AI badges, money bags, 3D glossy icons, neon glow, confetti, fake Airbnb inbox screenshots.

Keep graphics **sparse**. White space wins.

## Sections (one job each)

1. **Who it’s for**  
   Owners who want predictable cost, stay hands-on on the property, and offload messaging (and optionally pricing). Not for owners who want full turnovers handled end to end (point to Full Service / Growth).

2. **How Essentials is different** (**scope split graphic**)  
   Clear “MRG vs you” boundary. Lead with predictability: no % of revenue on this plan.

3. **Two tiers** (**price ladder**)  
   - **Message & Book — $199 / mo:** AI + VA guest messaging, booking confirmations, guest ID verification & screening, review requests, monthly performance snapshot  
   - **Message & Optimize — $349 / mo:** everything above, plus dynamic pricing, scheduled listing & photo optimization, monthly report with recommended next steps  
   Orange accent on the emphasized tier.

4. **What you keep**  
   Cleaning / turnovers, maintenance, inventory/restocking, insurance, taxes, property expenses. MRG may refer vendors but does not arrange or pay for repairs on this plan.

5. **Payment options**  
   Monthly, cancel anytime (30 days written notice). Optional **12-month Klarna** (or comparable) path at about **5% off**; no refunds on that annual path. Keep this calm and factual, not a finance promo billboard.

6. **Upgrade path**  
   Upgrade Message & Book → Message & Optimize, or move to Full Service / Growth, anytime by written request; new fees next billing cycle. Link `/full-service` and `/growth`.

7. **Proof**  
   Reuse hub numbers once: `$26,995` → `$33,713` · `+159%`. Toronto client, results vary. Chart-ready white band (engineering will mount interactive chart in orange).

8. **Fit checklist**  
   Want fixed monthly cost · will keep cleaning/ops · can grant platform access · open to a short call.

9. **FAQ**  
   What’s included at $199 vs $349 · is cleaning included (no) · HST · cancel anytime vs Klarna 12-mo · upgrading · furniture not on this plan · onboarding fee discussed on call · platforms / access required · areas.

10. **Final dark conversion band** `#222222`  
    Gold Book a call + coral audit + phone / email / WhatsApp.

11. **Footer**  
    Logo only (no duplicate wordmark text), plan links, `/makeover`, `/muskoka`, privacy, © 2026.
