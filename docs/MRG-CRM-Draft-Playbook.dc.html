Draft ≠ sent
Drafts never enter a bubble lane: full-width, dashed amber hairline, labelled “Draft — not sent”. Sent AI texts stay right-aligned with a green delivered line.
One current step
Only one step is emphasised at a time; done steps collapse to a single line. Owners are quiet mono labels — Hans or MRG — not assignee chips.
Same checklist for AI and humans
Each draft cites the step it serves, so approving a text and advancing the lead are the same motion.
