Property Management Agreement
Between Mandel Realty Group (“Manager”) and Lubna Khalid (“Owner”), for the short-term rental management of King St Condo, London, effective on the date of the Owner’s signature below.
1 · Appointment
The Owner appoints the Manager as exclusive agent to market, book, and service the property for short-term stays, and to communicate with guests on the Owner’s behalf for the duration of this agreement.
2 · Management fee
The Manager retains a fee calculated as a percentage of net rental revenue collected in each calendar month, as set out in Schedule A. Payouts to the Owner are issued monthly with an accompanying statement in the owner portal.
3 · Owner obligations
The Owner shall maintain the property in habitable condition, keep buildings insurance in force, and disclose any building or strata restrictions affecting short-term occupancy.
Continues on page 2 — Term and termination

4 · Term and termination
This agreement runs for an initial term of twelve months and continues month to month thereafter. Either party may end it with thirty days’ written notice; confirmed guest reservations are honoured through their stay.
